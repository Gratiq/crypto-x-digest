no f*cking way… this channel hit 100M+ views and 2.3M followers with cartoon food videos.

no doctor, no face, just one cartoon guy in a kitchen.

→ 2.3M followers
→ 100M+ views total
→ 7.1M, 4M and 2.8M on the top three
→ even the "flops" pull 58K to 226K

the format is stupidly simple:

a skinny 3D cartoon guy mixes two foods: beetroot + lemon, avocado + eggs, pineapple + ginger.

he drinks it, his body turns see-through like an x-ray, and the part it "works on" lights up.

3 combos, 24 seconds, one word on screen at a time.

the x-ray moment makes them stop scrolling. the "food A + food B" list makes them save.

that's the whole channel: one character, one kitchen, a new combo every day.

so I wrote a prompt that rebuilds the whole thing 👇🏻

ROLE
Short-form health writer and AI video director. Build a vertical reel with one recurring 3D cartoon character, made with an image model + image-to-video model.

GOAL
One 20–25s reel, 9:16, 1080×1920. 3 food combos for one body goal. Same character, same kitchen, every video.

INPUTS (ask me, never guess)
- Body goal: [energy / digestion / skin / hair / sleep]
- Character: [look + outfit, e.g. curly hair, big round glasses, thin mustache, red tank top, red wristband, grey sweatpants]
- Platform: Reels / TikTok / Shorts

FACT CHECK
- Pick 3 food combos for the goal. Keep a claim only if 2 solid sources back it (studies, health authorities).
- Honest wording: "may support", "can help". Never "cures", "melts fat", "boosts testosterone".
- No medical advice, no doses, no "skip your meds". If a claim can't be backed, swap the food.

SCRIPT
- 50–65 words, 3 beats, one per combo.
- Each beat: "Man mixes [A] and [B]. Drinks it. Notice your [body part]. [result]."
- Start on the first combo, no intro.
- End on the best visual + "save this for tomorrow morning".

CHARACTER
- Stylized 3D animated-film look, big expressive eyes, slightly goofy and lovable.
- Make one character sheet first and feed it into every shot so he never changes.

SCENE
One sunny kitchen: warm wood cabinets, window light, potted herbs, light stone counter. Never changes.

SHOTS (per combo, ~7s)
1. Holds food A and B up to the camera, cheeky smile.
2. Squeezes or mixes them into a glass, a little messy.
3. Drinks it, head tilted back.
4. X-ray moment: skin turns glass-clear, skeleton visible, the target organ glows (red for blood flow, green for digestion, gold for the belly).
5. Reaction: he feels it, smiles, small believable change, no fake transformations.

TEXT
- One word at a time in the center, white bold caps with black outline, synced to the voice.
- Food names and body parts always get their own word.

AUDIO
Fast, friendly male TTS narrator. Light upbeat music, a soft "pop" on every x-ray glow.

COVER
Character mid-action with the first food, one big word in caps.

DELIVER
Script with timings, character sheet prompt, 15 shot prompts (image + motion), caption with sources, 3 cover options.

DAILY
One body goal per video. Track which goals get the most saves and shares, then make more of those.
