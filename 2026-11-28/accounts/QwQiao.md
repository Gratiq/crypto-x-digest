## 2026-11-27 17:00 UTC · пост

widespread misconceptions about ai breaking crypto (cryptography):

1. if ai proves n = np, crypto is automatically broken.

2. as ai gets smarter, sooner or later it’ll break all cryptography.

the reality:

1. most likely p ≠ np

2. even if p = np, the proof may not be constructive, ie, it doesn’t hand you an actual algorithm

3. even if it does, it could be too slow to ever run. it’s only efficient in the academia not in the real world.

4. same can be said of the alleged breakthrough involving rsa.

5. breaking rsa doesn’t automatically break everything else, including ecc (which bitcoin uses).

6. the leading conjecture is that some problems are truly hard and no efficient solution exist in the universe. ai can’t find something that doesn’t exist no matter how smart it is.
